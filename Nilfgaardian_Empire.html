THE NILFGAARDIAN EMPIRE

"The Sun Shines Bright Upon Nilfgaard"

The Dominion & Greatest Civilization

Ceas'raet — The most powerful Empire in the history of the Continent.

The Nilfgaardian Empire is situated in the southern part of the Continent and is renowned as the primary economic and military powerhouse. Unlike the fragmented Northern Realms, Nilfgaard is forged upon strict legal discipline and centralized imperial leadership.

Its imperial territory expands through the subjugation of foreign realms, transforming them into prosperous provinces governed under the direct oversight of stewards or local vassals who have sworn allegiance.

"Only those born in the heart of the Empire are true Nilfgaardians, yet the entire continent shall bow to its supreme order."

Invasion Track Record (Northern Wars)

Three major military expeditions of the Empire to subjugate the Northern territories.

1st Northern War (1263)

📍 Location: Cintra, Upper Sodden, & Sodden Hill

Nilfgaard decisively conquered the Kingdom of Cintra, breaching the River Yaruga before ultimately being halted by the Northern Coalition Alliance at Sodden Hill.

2nd Northern War (1267–1268)

📍 Location: Rivia, Aedirn, & Battle of Brenna

A full-scale invasion commanded by Emperor Emhyr var Emreis, deploying over 300,000 troops to shatter the enemy defenses.

3rd Northern War (1272–Present)

📍 Location: Temeria, Aedirn, Velen, & Pontar Region

A swift, tactical campaign exploiting civil unrest in the North. Nilfgaard secured control over the Southern lands and gained strategic strongholds in Velen & Vizima.
