THE SKELLIGE ISLES

"For Freya and the Honor of the Isles!"

The High Realm & Mighty Clans

An unconquerable archipelago forged by sea, ice, and warrior blood.

The Skellige Isles are an isolated, mountainous archipelago located off the western coast of the Continent. Unlike the centralized empires of the mainland, Skellige is ruled through a federation of proud warrior clans bound by ancient tradition and honor.

Governance is shared between the **High King** (elected by the Jarls at Kaer Trolde) and powerful noble families such as Clan an Craite, Clan Drummond, and Clan Tuirseach. They honor the goddess Freya and fiercely defend their sea-bound freedom.

"The mainlanders think we are mere pirates. They forget that the ocean obeys only those who do not fear to bleed."

Invasion & Defense History

Centuries of naval raiding and fierce resistance against mainland conquest.

The Massacre of Cintra (1263)

📍 Location: Cintra & Yaruga Coastline

Bound by royal marriage to Queen Calanthe, Skellige warriors under King Eist Tuirseach fought valiantly against Nilfgaard's initial invasion, cementing an eternal blood feud.

The Ocean Raids on the Empire (1267–1268)

📍 Location: Great Sea & Southern Coastal Forts

Crach an Craite deployed the dreaded Skellige longship fleet to harass Nilfgaardian supply lines, burning imperial ports and thwarting naval supremacy in the South.

The Battle of Undvik & Wild Hunt Defense (1272)

📍 Location: Undvik & Kaer Trolde Bay

The united clans assembled their legendary warriors to defend their homeland from legendary threats and imperial encroachment alike.
