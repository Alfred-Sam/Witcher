About the Redanian Empire

The Center of Order, Progress, and Formidable Military Might.

The Redanian Empire stands firm as a mighty realm, built upon years of history, strategic victories, and unwavering unity.

Stretching between majestic mountain ranges and fertile plains, Redania stands as a beacon of order and civilized progress. Guided by strict laws and a supreme martial spirit.

"Honor, devotion, and soldierly fortitude are the eternal pillars supporting the majesty of the Redanian Throne."
